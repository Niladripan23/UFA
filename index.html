GAME GUIDE

UFA™ Auction Rules

01

Create or Join

Hosts create a private room and choose a 4–6 club capacity, starting budget and player-pool category. Friends join with the room code and password.

02

Build a Complete Squad

Each club can draft 1 Manager, 4 Forwards, 3 Midfielders, 3 Defenders and 1 Goalkeeper.

03

Beat the Hammer

Every live lot runs on a 12-second bidding clock. A new valid bid resets the clock, while players can pass when they are done contesting the lot.

04

Bid Slabs

Bid increments scale with the current price: +2 below 50, +5 from 50 through 100, and +10 above 100.

05

Draft Order

The auction moves through Managers, Midfielders, Forwards, Goalkeepers and Defenders, with short reveal intervals between lots.

THE GAME

The Idea Behind UFA™

UFA™ is built around the part of football that can be just as tense as the match itself: deciding who to sign, how much to spend, when to push a bid and when to walk away.

Create a private auction room, bring in your friends and build a complete squad under a shared clock. Every expensive win changes what you can afford later, so the auction becomes a game of timing, budgeting and reading your opponents.
